Essays on Infrastructure-as-Code

A collection of essays on repeatable infrastructure of all sizes and types for software engineering projects. It avoids the lengthy manuals and distils the essence of the standards and best practices that serve professionals in the field, bringing together different perspectives on a discipline that has become as treasured as any high-value asset.

Each essay throws a distinct perspective and leaves out the chores so the tenets stand out — spanning architecture, automation, naming conventions, compliance, technical debt, and emerging trends such as generative AI. Set in a lucid, narrative tone, the collection paints the landscape and history of software infrastructure engineering around automated deployments, and strives to be an easy read.

Algorithms!

Don't lose your interview again! Learn the tricks of the trade.

The difference between a junior software developer and a senior one can be seen from their communication on subject matter. During technical interviews, an interviewer may ask about something like bloom filters and leave an unprepared candidate stumped. Domain knowledge is one of the curveballs during the technical interviews.

This book is a summary cheat sheet for the most often encountered interview questions. It focuses on practical, contemporary algorithms relevant to technical interviews: no purely academic-interest algorithms to distract your focus, only what's relevant to the interviews. The format features clear descriptions without code samples, so you can build the credibility you need during technical discussions.
